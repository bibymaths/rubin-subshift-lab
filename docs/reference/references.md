# References

The implementation relies on standard definitions from symbolic dynamics, cellular automata, and permutation-group theory. The project does not attach theorem numbers or proof-grade claims to unsourced algorithms.

- M. Rubin, “On the reconstruction of topological spaces from their groups of homeomorphisms,” *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society* 312 (1989), 487–538.
- D. Lind and B. Marcus, *An Introduction to Symbolic Dynamics and Coding*, Cambridge University Press.
- T. Ceccherini-Silberstein and M. Coornaert, *Cellular Automata and Groups*, Springer.

These references motivate terminology. Current implementations should be assessed through their documented tests and guarantee labels, not by assuming they implement every result in the cited works.
