# Reconstruction Laboratory

The built-in comparison computes periodic and least-period counts for the binary full shift and golden mean shift over identical periods. It produces a finite signature heatmap and an invariant-by-invariant discrepancy chart.

Use the result language literally:

- **distinguished by the selected finite invariants**: at least one selected vector differs;
- **not distinguished within the tested bounds**: selected vectors agree, but untested invariants or larger periods may differ.
