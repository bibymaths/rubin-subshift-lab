# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free research-software community. Be constructive, critique ideas rather than people, respect differing experience levels, and make mathematical assumptions explicit.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information. Project maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact channel.
